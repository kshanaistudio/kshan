Frequently Asked Questions

KSHAN is an AI-powered photo delivery platform for events and photographers. It uses facial recognition to help guests instantly find their photos, delivers images in minutes via Camera2Cloud, and provides beautiful cloud galleries with WhatsApp integration.

KSHAN offers a free tier for guests to search and view their photos. Photographers and businesses can sign up for affordable plans. No hidden charges.

All facial recognition is done on-device. Biometric data is never stored or sold. Photos are stored securely on Cloudflare R2 with enterprise-grade encryption.

KSHAN is designed for professional event photographers, event planners, wedding hosts, corporate organisations, and anyone who wants to deliver event photos to guests instantly.

Simply sign up for a free account, create your first event, upload photos, and share the QR code or link with your guests. No app downloads required for guests.
